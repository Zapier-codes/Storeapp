Vyxel Apps is an open-source Android app store that scans six sources and merges them into one feed: GitHub, GitLab, F-Droid, IzzyOnDroid, APKPure and Aptoide. No ads and no bloat.

Find what you want faster
- Browse curated categories plus smart sections such as Trending and Newly Launched.
- See screenshots pulled straight from each project's README.
- Sync your GitHub starred repositories into your favourites.

Install with confidence
- Every downloaded APK is checked against the signing certificate of the app already on your phone before it installs, so a hijacked repository or redirected release cannot silently replace what you have.
- Your install history is kept, and you can roll back to a previous version from it.

Made for everyone
- Available in 16 languages, including English, Hindi, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Portuguese, Arabic and Chinese.
- Dismissible in-app announcements for releases and community updates.
- Optional Liquid Glass Pro themes.

Vyxel Apps is open source under the AGPL-3.0 licence.
